# Tools

Browser-based tools for adopting instructors. Each one runs entirely in your browser:
nothing you upload is sent to a server, and no account is required.

</div>

<div class="grid cards cards-2x2" markdown>

-   ![Canvas](../assets/canvas.png){ .card-logo } **[Canvas Rewriter](canvas-rewriter.md)**

    ---

    Upload a Canvas common-cartridge export (`.imscc` or `.zip`) and rewrite the
    JupyterHub and materials-repository links to point at your own hub and fork before
    importing it into Canvas.

-   ![Otter](../assets/otter.png){ .card-logo } **[Otter Service In-Browser](otter-grader.md)**

    ---

    The in-browser version of Otter Service Standalone. Upload an assignment's
    `autograder.zip` and the student notebooks, add a batch per assignment, and download a
    grades CSV for each, with no login and no download code.

</div>
